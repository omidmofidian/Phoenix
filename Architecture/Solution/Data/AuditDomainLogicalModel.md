# 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical logical representation of the Audit Domain within the Phoenix Platform.

The Audit Domain provides enterprise-wide traceability by recording business events, data changes and operational activities. It supports governance, compliance and historical accountability without affecting business behavior.

The model is technology independent and serves as the logical foundation for audit persistence.

---

# 2. Scope

## Included Aggregates

- Audit Event
- Audit Session

## Excluded Aggregates

- Business entities from the Reference Domain
- Business entities from the Core Domain
- Business entities from the Market Domain
- Integration entities

---

# 3. Architectural Role

The Audit Domain is a supporting domain responsible for enterprise traceability.

It records business events generated by other domains but never owns or modifies business entities.

Audit information is append-only and must remain historically consistent.

# 5. Aggregate Specifications

## 5.1 Audit Event

### Business Purpose

Represents a single auditable event generated by the platform.

### Business Responsibilities

- Record business events.
- Record data changes.
- Record security events.
- Preserve event history.
- Support enterprise traceability.

### Business Identifier

Audit Event Identifier

### Logical Characteristics

- Aggregate Root
- Immutable
- Append-only
- Historical entity

### Business Rules

- Audit Events are never updated.
- Audit Events are never deleted.
- Every Audit Event belongs to exactly one Audit Session.
- Every Audit Event references one originating Aggregate Root.
- Event timestamps are immutable.

---

## 5.2 Audit Session

### Business Purpose

Represents a logical execution context containing multiple audit events.

### Business Responsibilities

- Group related events.
- Maintain execution context.
- Support end-to-end traceability.

### Business Identifier

Audit Session Identifier

### Logical Characteristics

- Aggregate Root
- Supporting entity

### Business Rules

- One Audit Session may contain multiple Audit Events.
- Audit Sessions are immutable after completion.
- Sessions preserve chronological order.

# 7. Domain Constraints

The following logical constraints govern the Audit Domain:

- Audit data is append-only.
- Audit records shall never modify business entities.
- Every Audit Event references exactly one originating Aggregate Root.
- Audit history must remain complete and chronologically consistent.
- Business entity deletion shall not invalidate historical audit records.

---

# 8. Domain Invariants

The following invariants shall always hold:

- Every Aggregate has exactly one Aggregate Root.
- Audit information is immutable.
- Audit references target Aggregate Roots only.
- Historical integrity is preserved.
- Business semantics remain technology independent.
