# Business Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Market** | A financial marketplace representing a distinct trading ecosystem, such as the Iran Stock Market, Forex Market, or Cryptocurrency Market. A Market contains one or more Exchanges. |
| **Exchange** | A regulated trading venue where financial instruments are listed and traded. An Exchange belongs to exactly one Market and may operate multiple Trading Boards. |
| **Trading Board** | A logical subdivision of an Exchange representing a specific trading environment, market segment, or listing category. |
| **Instrument** | A tradable financial instrument recognized by the Phoenix Platform, including equities, ETFs, bonds, derivatives, currencies, cryptocurrencies, and future instrument types. |
| **Listing** | The relationship between an Instrument and the Exchange or Trading Board on which it is listed for trading. |
| **Symbol** | The unique trading identifier assigned to an Instrument within a specific Exchange or Trading Board. |
| **Market Data** | Raw market information collected from external sources, including prices, volume, order statistics, corporate events, and other trading-related information. |
| **Daily Market Data** | Canonical daily OHLCV market data stored by the platform after validation and normalization. |
| **Indicator** | A derived analytical metric calculated from market data using predefined mathematical or statistical formulas. |
| **Feature** | A normalized analytical attribute derived from one or more indicators or market data elements for use by scoring, prediction, or machine learning models. |
| **Prediction** | A probabilistic estimation generated by analytical or machine learning models regarding future market behavior. |
| **Signal** | A recommendation produced by the platform indicating a potential Buy, Sell, Hold, or other investment action based on analytical models and business rules. |
| **Position** | The current ownership status of an Instrument within a portfolio, including quantity, entry price, unrealized profit/loss, and risk exposure. |
| **Trade** | The complete lifecycle of an executed transaction from entry through final exit, including all related execution events. |
| **Portfolio** | A managed collection of Positions representing the current investment holdings of a user or strategy. |
| **Strategy** | A reusable set of business rules, analytical models, and decision logic used to generate investment Signals. |
| **Backtest** | The process of evaluating a Strategy using historical market data to measure its expected performance. |
| **Data Provider** | An external organization or system supplying market data, reference data, financial information, or other external datasets. |
| **Data Source** | A specific feed, API, file, or service provided by a Data Provider. |
| **Reference Data** | Relatively stable enterprise master data shared across multiple business domains, including Exchanges, Sectors, Industries, Currencies, and Calendars. |
| **Aggregate** | A consistency boundary within the Domain Model that groups logically related entities under a single Aggregate Root. |
| **Aggregate Root** | The primary entity responsible for maintaining consistency and enforcing business rules within an Aggregate. |
| **Business Identifier** | A stable business-defined identifier that uniquely identifies an entity independently of its technical database identifier. |
| **Canonical Model** | The enterprise-standard representation of business concepts used consistently across all Phoenix services and domains. |
