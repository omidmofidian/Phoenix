# 1. Purpose

This document defines the execution plan for Sprint 3.

Sprint 3 transforms the approved Enterprise Business Architecture into the canonical Enterprise Information Model that will serve as the authoritative logical foundation for every future implementation within the Phoenix Platform.

This sprint focuses exclusively on technology-independent information modeling.

No implementation artifacts shall be produced.

---

# 2. Mission

Establish a complete, internally consistent, and fully traceable Enterprise Information Model for the Phoenix Platform.

The resulting model shall become the single authoritative reference for physical database design, software development, analytics, artificial intelligence services, and future platform evolution.

---

# 3. Planning Principles

Sprint 3 shall be executed according to the following principles:

* Business before technology.
* Logical before physical.
* Canonical before implementation.
* Enterprise standards before domain models.
* Traceability across every architectural artifact.
* One business concept shall have one canonical representation.
* Repository organization follows capability ownership.
* Every modeling decision shall be justified by business semantics.

---

# 4. Sprint Objectives

Sprint 3 shall achieve the following objectives:

* Complete the Enterprise Information Model.
* Complete logical modeling for every business domain.
* Complete the Enterprise Data Dictionary.
* Define canonical identifiers.
* Define enterprise relationships.
* Define business constraints.
* Produce Canonical ERD Version 1.0.
* Validate enterprise consistency.
* Establish the Sprint 3 Information Modeling Baseline.

# 8. Success Criteria

Sprint 3 is successful when:

* Every approved business concept has a logical representation.
* Every logical entity is documented.
* Every relationship is validated.
* Every identifier has an approved ownership.
* Enterprise Data Dictionary is complete.
* Canonical ERD is approved.
* Cross-domain consistency is verified.
* No implementation-specific artifacts exist.
* All artifacts are traceable to the approved Business Architecture.

# 10. Risks

Potential risks include:

* Inconsistent terminology.
* Duplicate business concepts.
* Missing enterprise relationships.
* Invalid ownership boundaries.
* Technology leakage into logical models.
* Incomplete traceability.
* Cross-domain inconsistencies.

Mitigation shall follow the approved governance process.

---

# 11. Definition of Done

Sprint 3 shall be considered complete when:

* Every planned domain has been logically modeled.
* Enterprise Information Model has been approved.
* Enterprise Data Dictionary has been completed.
* Canonical ERD Version 1.0 has been approved.
* Architecture validation has completed successfully.
* Sprint Review has been approved.
* Sprint Baseline has been established.
* Sprint Closure has been formally recorded.

# 12. Transition to Sprint 4

Sprint 4 begins only after formal approval of the Sprint 3 Baseline.

Sprint 4 transforms the approved Enterprise Information Model into the physical PostgreSQL implementation.

Sprint 4 includes:

* Physical Data Model
* Schema Design
* Table Design
* Constraints
* Indexes
* Physical Optimization
* Database Bootstrap
* SQL Implementation
