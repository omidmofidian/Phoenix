# 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical business vocabulary of the Phoenix Platform.

It establishes a shared language for business stakeholders, architects, developers, data engineers, and machine learning components.

Each business term shall have one authoritative definition.

---

# 2. Scope

This glossary applies to all architectural, design, implementation, and operational artifacts of the Phoenix Platform.

Every document shall use the canonical terminology defined herein.

---

# 3. Glossary Structure

Business terms are organized into the following domains:

- Market Concepts
- Trading Concepts
- Portfolio Concepts
- Analytics Concepts
- Machine Learning Concepts
- Platform Concepts

---

# 4. Market Concepts

## Market

**Definition**

A regulated or organized environment where financial instruments are traded.

---

## Exchange

**Definition**

An organization that operates one or more financial markets and facilitates trading.

---

## Instrument

**Definition**

A tradable financial asset such as a stock, ETF, bond, commodity, currency pair, cryptocurrency, or derivative.

---

## Symbol

**Definition**

The identifier used by an exchange or market to represent a tradable instrument.

---

## Listing

**Definition**

The association of an instrument with a specific exchange or market.

---

## Trading Session

**Definition**

A defined period during which an exchange accepts and matches orders.

---

## Market Data

**Definition**

Information published by a market, including prices, volumes, and trading activity.

---

## Daily Market Data

**Definition**

Aggregated end-of-day market information for a tradable instrument, including open, high, low, close, volume, and related metrics.

---

# 5. Trading Concepts

## Strategy

**Definition**

A defined set of business rules used to identify trading opportunities.

---

## Signal

**Definition**

A recommendation or indication generated by analytical models suggesting a potential trading action.

---

## Position

**Definition**

The current holding of a financial instrument within a portfolio.

---

## Order

**Definition**

An instruction to buy or sell a financial instrument.

---

## Trade

**Definition**

The completed execution of a buy or sell order.

---

## Risk

**Definition**

The potential for financial loss associated with an investment decision.

---

# 6. Portfolio Concepts

## Portfolio

**Definition**

A collection of financial positions managed as a single investment entity.

---

## Account

**Definition**

A logical or financial container through which portfolios and transactions are managed.

---

## Organization

**Definition**

A business entity that owns accounts, portfolios, or platform resources.

---

# 7. Analytics Concepts

## Feature

**Definition**

A measurable attribute derived from market or business data and used as input for analytical or machine learning models.

---

## Indicator

**Definition**

A calculated metric derived from market data to support technical analysis.

---

## Factor

**Definition**

A quantitative variable believed to influence asset returns or investment performance.

---

## Score

**Definition**

A normalized numerical value representing the quality or attractiveness of an investment opportunity.

---

## Ranking

**Definition**

The ordered evaluation of investment opportunities according to predefined criteria.

---

# 8. Machine Learning Concepts

## Model

**Definition**

A statistical or machine learning algorithm trained to produce predictions or classifications.

---

## Prediction

**Definition**

The estimated outcome generated by an analytical or machine learning model.

---

## Training Dataset

**Definition**

A dataset used to train a machine learning model.

---

## Validation Dataset

**Definition**

A dataset used to evaluate model performance during development.

---

## Inference

**Definition**

The process of applying a trained model to generate predictions from new data.

---

# 9. Platform Concepts

## Service

**Definition**

An independently deployable software component responsible for a specific business capability.

---

## Domain

**Definition**

A bounded business area representing a coherent set of concepts and responsibilities.

---

## Provider

**Definition**

An external system supplying market, financial, or reference data.

---

## Integration

**Definition**

The process of exchanging information between Phoenix and external systems.

---

## Configuration

**Definition**

A managed set of parameters controlling system behavior.

---

# 10. Governance Rules

- Every business concept shall have one canonical definition.
- Synonyms may be documented but shall not replace the canonical term.
- Business definitions shall remain technology-independent.
- All new terms require architectural review before approval.
- Deprecated terms shall remain documented for historical traceability.
