# 1. Purpose

This standard defines the canonical design rules for all Master Entities
within the Phoenix Platform.

The objective is to ensure that every master entity follows a consistent,
predictable, self-descriptive, and maintainable structure across the entire
repository.

This standard applies to every business entity regardless of market
(Iran Stock Exchange, Forex, Cryptocurrency, Commodities, etc.).

---

# 2. Scope

This standard applies to:

- Exchange
- Market
- Board
- Instrument
- Instrument Listing
- Market Index
- Currency
- Sector
- Industry
- Country
- Province
- City
- Broker
- Data Source
- Any future Master Entity

It does not apply to:

- Fact Tables
- Bridge Tables
- Transaction Tables
- Event Tables
- Audit Tables
- Log Tables

---

# 3. Design Principles

Every master entity shall:

- Have a single business responsibility.
- Represent one business concept.
- Be independent from analytical data.
- Store master data only.
- Never store historical facts.
- Never store calculated values.
- Support long-term evolution.

---

# 4. Naming Convention

Generic column names are prohibited.

The following names SHALL NOT be used:

- id
- code
- name
- symbol
- description
- status
- type
- local_name
- short_name

Every column name shall begin with the entity name.

Example:

Exchange

exchange_id

exchange_code

exchange_name

exchange_local_name

ExchangeStatus

exchange_status_id

MarketIndex

market_index_id

market_index_code

market_index_symbol

market_index_name

market_index_local_name

market_index_short_name

---

# 5. Primary Key

Every master entity shall contain:

<entity>_id

Example:

instrument_id

market_index_id

currency_id

---

# 6. Public Identifier

Every master entity shall contain:

public_id UUID

Generated by:

gen_random_uuid()

---

# 7. Business Identifier

Every entity should contain a stable business identifier.

Examples:

instrument_code

exchange_code

market_code

currency_code

market_index_code

---

# 8. Business Name

Every entity shall contain:

<entity>_name

Optional:

<entity>_local_name

<entity>_short_name

---

# 9. Status

Business status shall always be represented using reference tables.

Example:

instrument_status_id

exchange_status_id

market_status_id

Boolean status flags are allowed only for lifecycle control.

Example:

is_active

---

# 10. Audit Columns

Every master entity shall include:

created_at

created_by

updated_at

updated_by

version

No exceptions.

---

# 11. Foreign Keys

Foreign keys shall reference canonical master tables.

No duplicated business attributes shall be stored.

---

# 12. Business Rules

Master entities:

shall not contain prices.

shall not contain quantities.

shall not contain transactions.

shall not contain market data.

shall not contain analytical results.

---

# 13. Repository Consistency

Every new master entity must comply with this standard before approval.

No exception shall be introduced without an Architecture Decision Record (ADR).

---
